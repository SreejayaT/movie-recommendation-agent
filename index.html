Picks backed by what reviewers actually said.

Star ratings tell you a film is good. They don't tell you it's slow, sad, funny or strange. Reelwise reads real reviews, finds the ones that match what you're in the mood for, and recommends a movie or series with the evidence attached.

Describe a mood, get a pick

Pick an example above or type a mood. Highlighted phrases are the parts of each review that led to the pick.

This page runs a scripted demo with sample review text written for illustration. The real agent calls the Groq API and OMDb from a backend, so no API keys ever sit in this page.

Why not just use ratings?

Ratings only

"7.9 out of 10." You learn that people liked it, but not whether it suits a quiet Sunday or a loud Friday.

Ratings plus review text

The agent matches your words to the language reviewers use ("slow burn", "comforting", "gorgeous to look at") and shows you the lines behind its choice.

How a recommendation happens

  1. Collect reviews

    Real movie and series reviews are loaded into a database, each tagged with its title.

  2. Index the review text

    Reviews are split into passages and turned into embeddings so they can be searched by meaning, not just keywords.

  3. Understand the request

    The agent reads what you typed and decides which tools it needs.

  4. Retrieve and look up

    It pulls the most relevant review passages, then asks OMDb for ratings and basic details on the candidates.

  5. Answer from the evidence

    A Groq-hosted LLM writes the recommendation using only the retrieved reviews, and quotes the lines that support it.
